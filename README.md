# Biblioteca Viva

**Borrow a Master, Keep the Tradition.** A living library of Madeiran skills, music and stories. Hackathon demo, 2026-11-20, demo at 16:00.

**Live demo:** https://mestre-library.emergent.host/

## The idea in one line
You borrow a grandpa or grandma *and* the tools of their craft. They teach you a Madeiran skill, the session is filmed with borrowed gear, and the lesson stays in the library for the next learner.

## Three shelves
1. **Borrow a Mestre** — older islanders listed like library books: skill, short life story, village, availability. 45–60 min sessions at a Casa do Povo, a café, or the Mestre's village.
2. **Borrow the tools** — instrument, embroidery hoop, stone griddle, fishing rod. Every loan includes a filming kit (tripod, mic, light).
3. **The Living Archive** — each filmed lesson becomes a short video with PT/EN subtitles, tagged by skill, Mestre and village.

**Library card:** a stamp per skill learned. Three sessions with the same Mestre = "graduated", borrow the tools for a week.

## Users
- **Mestres** — older Madeirans, shown as experts. They do not use the app; a coordinator books for them (a phone call beats an app).
- **Learners** — local teens, tourists, digital nomads.
- **Creators** — TikTok/YouTube/Instagram creators who want sessions for content, with the Mestre's consent, credit and revenue share.
- **Coordinator** — approves bookings and tool loans, marks sessions completed.

## Demo scope (phase 1)
Working flow: browse → book with tools → coordinator approves → mark completed → stamp appears on card → watch an archive lesson.

- Mestre catalogue (~8 Mestres, one per skill), filter by skill/village
- Booking with tool loan + filming kit, starts "Pending"
- Coordinator queue: Approve / Decline / Mark completed (tools returned)
- Library card with stamps and graduation
- Living Archive with PT/EN subtitle excerpts
- Creator consent card (credit, use: personal/social/commercial, revenue share)
- PT/EN switch and large-text mode in the header, always visible
- Role switcher (Learner / Coordinator), no passwords

**Not in phase 1:** real sign-in, payments, real uploads, AI subtitles, WhatsApp/phone integration. Pitch these as "next".

## Design rules for older users
18px+ text, at most three actions per screen, no hidden menus, big tap targets. "Learn together", never "help for the elderly". Warm island-library look: off-white paper, stamp and library-card details, Atlantic blue, volcanic black, laurel green, bougainvillea pink accent.

## Roles today
- **Person 1 — Pitch:** 3-min pitch (problem, three shelves, demo, business model, next steps), research checklist with sources, unit economics for one session, slides, Q&A, presents.
- **Person 2 — Features:** owns the app and is the only one who changes it. No new features after 14:15. Adds Person 3's content, tests on laptop and phone, records a 60-second backup video, drives the demo.
- **Person 3 — Content (GenAI):** 8 Mestre profiles (name, village, skill, short story, PT+EN), 6–8 clips of 15–30s, AI portraits and thumbnails, title + PT/EN subtitle excerpt per clip. Every clip labelled "AI-generated example". Never imitate a real identifiable person. Optional 20s vision teaser.

GenAI tips: one shared style prompt (warm light, Madeira's terraces and sea, older hands at work) so the clips look like one library. Portraits first, then use them as video references. Keep clips short; backup is a still image with narration.

## Demo script (2–3 min)
1. The problem: Portugal's ageing population, loneliness, Madeiran traditions fading.
2. Optional: open with the teaser clip.
3. Creator Rita picks the machete Mestre, books a session; machete and filming kit are added.
4. She ticks the consent boxes; the consent card appears.
5. Switch to Coordinator: approve, then mark completed. A stamp appears on Rita's card.
6. Living Archive: play an AI example lesson, switch PT/EN.
7. Business model slide, "Next" slide, close with *"Borrow a Master, Keep the Tradition."*

## Business model (starting figures, to check)
- Tourists: €25–40 per session, 60% to the Mestre, 40% to operations.
- Creators: session fee + commercial licence, share to the Mestre.
- Hotels and tour operators: "local experience" packages (main B2B channel to test).
- Teens and students: free or volunteer hours, funded by the paid side and grants (EU ageing and culture programmes, Madeira regional government, municipal funds).
- Partners: Casas do Povo and juntas de freguesia (venues, tool depots), Universidade da Madeira (volunteers, research).

## Research checklist
1. Verify rajão, despique, machete/braguinha, Camacha wicker; find associations to contact.
2. How Casas do Povo and juntas work, and whether they lend rooms.
3. Ponta do Sol Digital Nomad Village and the size of Madeira's nomad community.
4. Madeira tourist numbers; prices for "authentic experiences" (Airbnb Experiences, GetYourGuide).
5. Portugal and Madeira ageing and loneliness statistics.
6. Similar models: Human Library, Grandmas Project, Nonna Live, Library of Things.
7. Legal: Mestre income vs pension and benefits, image consent (GDPR), tool-loan insurance.
8. Funding: EU / Portugal 2030, Erasmus+, regional cultural grants.

## Next steps after the hackathon

3. Film the first sessions — they become the first Archive videos and the pitch material.

## Assumptions
Presentation demo only: users, bookings and payments are simulated, no money moves, no messages sent. Mestre names, stories and portraits are invented and labelled as examples. Prices, the 60/40 split and funding sources are starting figures. English is the default; the Portuguese wording is a draft for a native speaker to review.
